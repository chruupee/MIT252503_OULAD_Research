The study provides evidence that early behavioural engagement data can be used to identify patterns associated with at-risk learning outcomes in digital learning environments. Predictive models achieved measurable discrimination in both OULAD and KDD Cup 2015, while explainability analysis identified activity and interaction measures as important contributors to predictions. The same directional engagement patterns were observed across both cohorts, and bidirectional transfer retained predictive discrimination. However, differences in outcome definitions, cohort distributions and predicted positive rates demonstrate that cross-cohort deployment requires cohort-specific calibration and validation. The findings therefore support the feasibility of early predictive identification while highlighting the importance of explainability, fairness assessment and external validation before practical deployment.
